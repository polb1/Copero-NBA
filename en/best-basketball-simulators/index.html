The simulators, one by one

1. NBA Legacy our game

A free basketball career simulator in your browser. Create your player at 18, pick a country and position, and live the whole career until retirement: youth club, NBA draft, contracts, injuries, trades, awards and legacy.

  • Real 2026-27 NBA rosters and real clubs across Europe and the world (Liga ACB, EuroLeague, Italy, Greece, Turkey…).
  • Decisions with consequences and clutch shots in mini-games.
  • Three paces: a full career in 5-10 minutes in the quick mode.
  • Free, no sign-up, no download; made for phones and installable as an app.

Pick it if: you want a player's career, from Europe to the NBA, in short sessions.

Play free

2. Basketball GM

The best-known free basketball management sim. You are the general manager: draft, trades, free agency, salary cap and finances, with rosters from any season in NBA history.

Pick it if: you'd rather build a champion than be a player. Site: basketball-gm.com

3. HoopDreams

A text-driven career sim that starts at 16 and runs through the draft, contracts and retirement. Career mode is free; a one-time paid upgrade unlocks the full season and removes the daily career limit.

Pick it if: you want lots of modes and duels with friends. Site: hoopdreams.io

4. DRAFTBOUND

A career that starts at 14, with high school, college recruiting, the draft and pro seasons in a fictional league (no real NBA teams). Free, with local saves.

Pick it if: you want to live the high-school years too. Site: draftboundsim.com

5. My NBA Career Simulator

Create a prospect, choose a pre-draft path (NCAA, G League, EuroLeague or Overtime Elite) and simulate up to 20 seasons at once or year by year. Free, no account.

Pick it if: you want a career's outcome in seconds. Site: mynbacareer.com

6. NBA 2K (MyCareer mode)

The official NBA video game. In MyCareer you play the games yourself with your 3D player. It's paid, and player progression is sped up with virtual currency.

Pick it if: you want to play the games with a controller and don't mind paying.

Which one should you pick?

  • Be a player and reach the NBA, for free: NBA Legacy, HoopDreams, DRAFTBOUND or My NBA Career Simulator.
  • Build a career in Europe: NBA Legacy.
  • Run a team: Basketball GM.
  • Play the games in 3D: NBA 2K.

FAQ

What is the best free basketball simulator?

It depends on what you want. To live a player's career in the browser, NBA Legacy is free, needs no sign-up and uses real 2026-27 rosters. To run a franchise, Basketball GM is the free reference. To play the games with console graphics, NBA 2K, which is paid.

Is there a basketball career simulator where you can play in Europe?

In NBA Legacy you can start at a club in your country, play in Spain's Liga ACB or the EuroLeague and jump to the NBA later, or stay in Europe for your whole career.

Is there a free alternative to NBA 2K MyCareer?

Yes, although you don't play the games in 3D: NBA Legacy, HoopDreams, DRAFTBOUND and My NBA Career Simulator are free career simulators you play in the browser and on your phone.
